# Welcome to BTObot 🏠

Your AI-powered guide to Singapore's **Build-To-Order (BTO)** housing scheme — built for young couples planning their first home.

## What I can help with

| Topic | Examples |
|---|---|
| **Eligibility** | Income ceiling, citizenship, family nucleus rules |
| **Grants** | EHG, CPF Housing Grant, Proximity Housing Grant |
| **Application** | How to ballot, flat selection, signing the lease |
| **Flat types** | 2-room Flexi, 3-room, 4-room, 5-room, differences |
| **Timelines** | Waiting times, key collection, resale levy |
| **Financing** | HDB loan vs bank loan, CPF usage, MSR/TDSR |
| **Prime/Plus** | PLH model, clawback rules, MOP differences |

## How it works

Each answer draws from a curated knowledge base of HDB official pages and trusted BTO guides.  
The **top 3 most relevant passages** are surfaced and cited — click the source panels beneath any answer to read the original excerpt.

> ⚠️ BTObot provides general guidance only. Always verify details directly on the [HDB website](https://www.hdb.gov.sg) or consult an HDB officer for your specific situation.
